01 — The reason this exists

A weld nobody can sign for is scrap.

Strip the industry down to its load-bearing rule and this is what is left: a welded joint is a process whose quality cannot be fully verified after the fact without destroying it. Standards bodies solved that by regulating the people instead of only the product.

So certification schemes require a named, qualified welding coordinator to take responsibility for procedures, consumables, welder qualification and inspection. Pressure equipment, structural steel, pipelines, offshore, rail, shipbuilding — the same logic everywhere.

The International Welding Engineer diploma is the highest of the international coordination levels. It is not a course you take to feel better qualified. It is the document a company must be able to point at.

According to the international standards it is a requirement for companies involved in the production of welded structures to employ a welding engineer with an IWE/EWE diploma. — University of Miskolc, programme description
  • The curriculum meets IIW and EWF requirements in full.
  • The diploma reads International Welding Engineer / European Welding Engineer — both marks, one course.
  • It is a personal qualification. It moves with you, between employers and between countries.

02 — What is actually taught

Four subjects. 480 hours. No filler.

361 hours of lectures and 119 hours of practical work, built to the IIW guideline for International Welding Engineers.

Module A

Materials and weldability

Metallurgy of the heat-affected zone, steel grades and their behaviour under thermal cycles, materials testing.

Module B

Processes and equipment

Arc, resistance and beam processes, consumables, power sources, automation and the parameters that decide the joint.

Module C

Design of welded structures

Joint design, stress and fatigue behaviour, distortion, residual stress, design codes for welded construction.

Module D

Fabrication and quality

Welding coordination itself: WPS and qualification, NDT, quality systems, health and safety, cost of quality.

You attend from where you are

Lectures run in hybrid form. Come to Miskolc if you can; follow them online if you cannot.

Two weeks in the workshop

Practicals are blocked: one week in semester one, one week in semester two, on campus, hands on the equipment.

Thesis and a final exam

The course closes with a degree thesis and a final examination held in person. Then the diploma is yours.

08 — Where this diploma is being used

Written for two markets in particular.

The Gulf, the Levant and North Africa

Energy and petrochemical contractors working to international fabrication standards need welding coordination on their approved-vendor documentation. IWE is the level that satisfies the top tier of that requirement, and it is a European qualification held by an individual, not tied to one employer.

09 — Questions people actually ask

Answers, without the brochure voice.

Do I have to move to Hungary?

No. Lectures run in hybrid form and can be followed online from anywhere. You must be in Miskolc for two block weeks of practical classes — one in semester one, one in semester two — and for the final examination, which is held in person.

What does it cost in total?

€2,000 per semester for three semesters, plus a €610 examination fee: €6,610. Travel, accommodation, insurance and visa costs are separate and depend on where you are coming from.

Which degrees are accepted?

Mechanical, materials, metallurgical, civil and energy engineering degrees qualify automatically. Gas and petroleum, engineering management, architecture, industrial product design, vehicle, logistics, mechatronics, economics engineering, technical teaching, chemical and IT engineering degrees are accepted after the faculty reviews your transcript for preliminary competences.

Is the diploma recognised outside Hungary?

The curriculum and syllabus meet the requirements of the International Institute of Welding and the European Welding Federation, and the award is issued as International Welding Engineer / European Welding Engineer. Recognition of a personal qualification by a specific client or national body is still their decision — check your target market's rules.

What English level is required?

IELTS 6.0 or an equivalent certificate, plus the online oral interview, which also tests whether you can hold a technical conversation in English.

Can I get a dormitory place?

Not currently — dormitory places have been fully allocated, and new applications are accepted only from applicants who arrange their own accommodation. Confirmed accommodation is a required part of the student visa application, so treat it as a blocking step.

What if the course doesn't run?

The University reserves the right not to launch the programme with fewer than ten applicants. In that case the tuition fee is refunded to the applicant.

Are there scholarships?

The University lists several Hungarian state schemes, including Stipendium Hungaricum. Each has its own eligibility rules, deadlines and application route, separate from the university application. See the scholarship programmes.

I'm from Bangladesh, Pakistan, Syria, Afghanistan or Yemen. Can I apply?

The University closed applications from those countries as of 30 April 2026 because of expected visa processing times. If your situation has changed, or you hold residency elsewhere, ask the admissions office directly at apply@uni-miskolc.hu rather than assuming either way.
